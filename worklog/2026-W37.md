### Key Findings
- **CI-2/CI-3 NAT gateway incident**: Both NAT gateways deleted by `sminonne` at 2026-09-04T19:34:50Z (1s apart). Route tables left with blackhole `0.0.0.0/0`. Impact: 29 HCs across both clusters stuck with `ValidReleaseImage=False`. CloudTrail confirms manual CLI commands from same session.
- **ROSA DR integrity analysis**: CSI Volume Snapshots are crash-consistent, not application-consistent — but "not application-consistent" ≠ "not recoverable." EBS snapshots + etcd WAL CRC + bbolt two-phase commit = fully recoverable by design. Document overstates risk.

- **[#9506](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/9506)** (OCPBUGS-120685): `getDefaultNodePool` latent bug — 28+ callers vulnerable to race with deleting NPs. Author hardened directly with `DeletionTimestamp` + `Replicas` guards.
- **RITS [#9489](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/9489)**: 6 presubmit failures on release-5.0 — only 2 (compliance test) caused by missing kas-connection-checker annotation.
