Disputed with empirical analysis — the failing CRR was stuck in `ensureOldSignerCertificateRevoked` (normal revocation path), not in the nil-resource guard clauses we modified. Revert closed without merge.
- **[release#81788](https://github.com/openshift/release/pull/81788)**: Reworked to tiered finalizer removal per @csrwng feedback. Pruner cron runs every 15min; each invocation evaluates deletion age and escalates: Tier 1 (>=30min) awsmachine + EC2, Tier 2 (>=1hr) HCP + CAPI, Tier 3 (>=1.5hr) CP namespace, Tier 4 (>=2hr) HC/NP finalizers.

- **CI pass rate**: 31% → 46% → 53% → 48%. Drop from 7d window rotation, not regression.
