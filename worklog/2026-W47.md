### Key Findings
- **[#10129](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/10129)** (ARO-30373): production-code reproducers and a native etcd cluster confirmed sidecar-only failures can select a serving member for deletion and lose quorum when another member is down; endpoint-health authorization is needed before destructive recovery.
- **[#10329](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/10329)** (OCPBUGS-141929): a release-metadata lookup error deletes the reported-version ConfigMap while the Deployment still references it, risking replacement-pod mount failures; CCO tests also panic because fixtures omit the user-release provider.
- **[#10036](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/10036)** (CNTRLPLANE-4639): the installation-failure metric's `ClusterVersionFailing` reason remains permanently zero because the HC exposes `ClusterVersionSucceeding`, while the scheduling-failure E2E still mutates shared management nodes.
- **[#10145](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/10145)** (OCPBUGS-111701): annotation-only CRI-O ulimit preservation can be lost after upgrade or resource recreation, changing rollout inputs; durable state and operator-upgrade E2E evidence were requested.
- **[#10288](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/10288)** (OCPBUGS-140686): the 13 Nov RCA reproduced the Default feature-gate/capability mismatch behind 126 CRD conformance failures and validated the merged fix, but that day's official nightly still contained an older CPO.
- **[test-results-verification#19](https://github.com/hypershift-community/test-results-verification/pull/19)** (OCPSTRAT-2653 / OCPSTRAT-3926): corrected Day 1 scope against merged code—annotations affect the primary endpoint, provider-prefix admission restrictions exist, service-list immutability remains, and the single delivered E2E is not wired into CI.
- **[release#88923](https://github.com/openshift/release/pull/88923)** (OCPBUGS-141889): the HA policy correction was approved, but the recorded follow-up found no requested rehearsal runs and Tide rejected the author allowlist; approval alone did not unblock delivery.
- **[#10118](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/pull/10118)** (OCPBUGS-140635 / ARO-30375): corrected scripts passed interrupted-recovery reproducers, but executable regression tests and caller-level coverage are still needed to prove unhealthy endpoints never authorize Pod/PVC deletion.
